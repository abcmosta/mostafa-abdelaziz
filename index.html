Career Highlights & Impact

Inventory Defect Reduction

26% ➔ 3%

The Problem

High inventory record defect rates caused physical quantities to drift away from system records, creating fulfillment delays and operational risk.

My Process

I designed SQL-based weekly and monthly counting systems, built ETL logic for trend analysis, and isolated the exact traffic and associate behaviors driving the defects.

The Result

Cut inventory record defects from 26% to 3%, strengthening inventory integrity and reducing rework across the fulfillment operation.

ERDR DPMO Optimization

530K ➔ 285K

The Problem

Virtual-physical expiration mismatches and operational defects were inflating DPMO in a high-volume Amazon fulfillment center.

My Process

I used defect-trend analysis, root-cause modeling, and targeted interventions to isolate error drivers and prioritize the highest-impact fixes.

The Result

Reduced ERDR DPMO from 530K to 285K quarter-over-quarter through structured process improvements and data-based execution.
